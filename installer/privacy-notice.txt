PDF Utility Suite privacy notice

PDF Utility Suite processes PDFs and images on your computer. The app opens the files you select and saves results to the locations you choose.

The app keeps local diagnostic logs with the app version, operation status, and error types. The logs omit document names, contents, and passwords. You can view the log path and open its folder from Settings. You decide whether to share a log when asking for help.

When you choose Check for Updates, the app contacts GitHub for release information. If you choose to download an update, your browser opens the download from GitHub.
